§ 07 · for curious minds of all ages

curious adj.

1.Eager to know what is inside things. Especially bins.

tap the bin and pull something out ↓

10 things inside. tap the bin!

how to play

rummage v.

There are ten things hiding in this Parisian bin. Each one has a story about rubbish — from ancient Rome to today. Tap the bin, pull them out one by one and become a real garbologist (yes, that's a real word: a person who studies rubbish!).

§ 07.2 — timeline

through time adv. phr.

Everything you pull out lands on its spot in history. Tap a find to read it again.

    where this comes from

    Facts checked against encyclopedias and public sources, for example Eugène Poubelle, Monte Testaccio, Chiffonnier and the City of Paris. Some old stories (like the king and the smelly mud) are legends that chroniclers loved to tell — we say so when it's the case.
